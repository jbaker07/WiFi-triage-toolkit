Remediation tips

  • RSSI worse than -65 dBm or SNR below 25 dB: move closer to the access point, reduce interference, prefer 5 GHz.
  • High packet loss or latency to the gateway: the problem is the Wi-Fi hop. Check channel congestion, try the other band, look for interference.
  • Clean gateway but loss or latency to DNS or the internet: the problem is past the access point. Check the uplink and any captive portal.
  • Slow DNS or TLS in the HTTP timing usually points at the network path or MTU. Compare wired against Wi-Fi.
